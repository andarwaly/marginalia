# A Reference note is one definition in the owner's vocabulary

Status: accepted.

The owner (2026-11-10) reviewed eight entries `define` wrote from one source note. "Where it is found" had become a location in the video, "How to use it" described the demo, and the four part entries repeated the same sentences. The definitions used the author's framing ("Marchese treats it as…") and left out the owner's own Stances. The owner's hand-written `Taste.md` is three neutral sentences in their vocabulary, with no author name, no opinion and no sections.

Changes:

- **Definition only.** The agent writes one to three sentences. It may offer a further heading with the note line behind it; the owner writes any heading.
- **The owner's words, neutral.** The definition draws on the owner's lines first, with no author name or first person. Agreement and disagreement stay in the source note.
- **Links in prose.** A term with a Reference note is linked at its first mention; a part of a larger term opens with "Part of [[Harness|harness]]".
- **No caveat line.** `sources` already shows a single source. "Write it anyway" holds for the session.
- **Input and form.** A source note from the session or one the owner names counts as notes; a vault template sets the frontmatter and filename; the `※` prefix is gone.

Considered: keep the sections when the notes support them (rejected: the agent filled them with whatever the notes held); use the owner's words with their opinions (rejected: a lookup should say what the term is). Risk: an entry reflects the owner's understanding at that moment and may conflict with a later source; `define` asks when notes conflict.
