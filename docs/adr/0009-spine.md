# The Spine: discuss the few things a source is about

Status: accepted. Amends 0007 (Stance, Offshoots) and the coverage rule that every example and claim passage gets a question.

The owner (2026-11-10) read a v9 session on a 16-minute video about harness engineering. It took 60 agent turns and about two hours, and produced 24 source points and 56 Key Concepts and Mentioned terms. The owner said: "something get repeated and make things blurred out. and we miss other thing as well." The video's formula had no source point, and its four layers appeared as one Key Concepts line. Every claim had equal weight, because the passage walk tagged each claim as an ask and nothing asked for the source's structure.

Changes:

- **Spine.** The agent drafts three to six items: core ideas and the structures the author names. The owner names what they think the source holds first, the agent adds what they missed, and the owner confirms. One item, one thread, one source point.
- **Ask passages** shrink to the passage stating an item and at most one example or support. Other passages are shown as mentions; repeats are skipped. The owner may take up any passage, and the reply goes under its item.
- **Stance** is asked only when the discussion holds no reasoned agreement or disagreement. 11 of the session's 20 Stance questions repeated a question already answered.
- **Asked once.** Before each question, the agent checks whether the owner has already answered it.
- **Repetition cuts.** A quoted opener only when it is the real link, at most one reply in four (25 of 60 turns opened `You said`). The Intention question once per session. One closing list.
- **Offshoots** may be kept as said. Dropping was the only alternative to rewriting, and it deleted the owner's thinking. The `ideate` suggestion moves after the file is written.
- **Terms** cap at about three per point and five for the whole source, excluding skipped passages.

Considered: keep threads and trim only the close (rejected: repetition was the symptom of equal weight); the agent proposes the Spine and the owner edits (kept only for a source the owner has not read, because it frames the owner's reading before they think); bring back a written main idea (rejected in 0006; the Spine steers the discussion, and the note still records the owner's words). Risk: the agent's draft decides what counts as "missed", and a sharp disagreement on a detail survives only if the owner raises that passage.
